## §0 The request
1. **First-person mode.** Camera at the fighter's head. All animations must be adjusted to make sense in first person.
2. **Preview bug (DONE by the architect):** the DOM ellipse `.gk-preview__pedestal` painted a black disc over the rotating model in the lobby and character-select previews — now hidden when the live 3D preview runs (`ui.css`).
3. **Ultimate overhaul.** Right now every ultimate is an AoE or a buff with a similar animation. Make each ultimate **specific to its animal and unique in function**; **more of them should target individuals**, with a **targeting system** (path/line indicators, or a lock-on range) that shows what will happen; some AoE may stay but with a **different, far more complex animation** than the old shared AoE feel. Ultimate animations must be smooth and worthy of an ultimate. Creative freedom is delegated to the architect (this doc).

## §2 Shared contract (ALREADY in `src/core/types.ts`; all additions optional/additive)
- `UltTargetKind = 'lock' | 'line' | 'ground' | 'self'`.
- `FighterState` optional: `ultPhase ('windup'|'active'|'recovery')`, `ultStage` (0-based beat index), `ultTargetId` (-1 none). The sim must fill them in `snapshot()` while an ultimate runs (rigs animate from them).
- `WorldSnapshot.projectiles?: ProjectileState[]` (`{id, kind:'boulder', pos, vel, radius, ownerId}`).
- Events: `ultimateTarget {fighterId, animal, kind, targetId, from, to, range, width, windup}` (emitted when the target resolves, at cast start), `ultimateFizzle {fighterId, reason:'noTarget'}`, `ultimateStage {fighterId, animal, stage, targetId, pos}`, `blink {fighterId, from, to}` (renderers must not interpolate across it), `projectileImpact {kind, pos, radius, ownerId, hitId}`. Existing `telegraph`/`ultimate` events stay.
- **Targeting resolution (sim-authoritative, deterministic, no intent changes):** at cast the sim resolves the target from `aimYaw`:
  - `lock`: candidates = alive, targetable, ground-reachable (unless `hitsAir`), body within `range`, inside a `coneDeg` cone (default 70°) around `aimYaw`; pick the best (smallest angle, then nearest). None → `ultimateFizzle`, **charge is NOT spent**, no cooldown. Lock-on/aim assist in MatchController already aim at the locked target, so the player's locked enemy wins the selection.
  - `line`: straight path along `aimYaw` (length `range`, width `width`), optional homing/steering (rhino).
  - `ground`: point along `aimYaw` clamped to `range` (snaps to an enemy on the line like the existing aim snap), zone `radius`.
  - `self`: none.
- Pure shared helper (created by WP-R0, used by sim, AI and the HUD preview): `src/sim/ultimates/targeting.ts` exporting at least `selectLockTarget(...)`, `previewUltTarget(spec, attackerState, fighterStates): { kind, valid, targetId, from: Vec3, to: Vec3, range, width }` (works on plain `FighterState` so the renderer can preview without the sim) and the `UltTargeting` config type (`{kind, range, coneDeg?, width?, radius?, requireTarget?, hitsAir?}`).

## §3 The ten new ultimates (design; numbers are starting points, tune with `npm run balance`)
Single-target ults ≈ 250–320 total damage on an unblocked victim; AoE/line ≈ 130–200 per victim; still cost 100 charge; ~1 cast per fighter per match at L3/L4 must hold; win-rate bands from BALANCE.md must hold (no animal outside ≈ 4–18% at L3/L4, ≤ ≈ 30% at L1/L2, 0 timeouts). Every ult must be **interruptible only in the ways stated**, must clean up (`endAbility`) on death/stun/round end, and respect `withinGroundReach` (v1.2 altitude filter) and traps' immunities where relevant.

|---|---|---|---|
| Lion | **Royal Hunt** | lock, 12 m | Locks eyes (reticle on the victim, arc path shown). Bounding pounce (~0.5 s arc) pins the target (60 dmg, knockdown ~0.6 s); 4 rapid maul strikes (guard-piercing, ~45 each, `ultimateStage` per strike) on the pinned victim; finishes with a face-to-face roar that **marks** them: +20% damage taken for 6 s, lion +20% speed 4 s. Lion is CC-immune while mauling. |
| Gorilla | **Boulder Hurl** | line/aim (range ~18 m, width ~1.6, lock-assisted) | Two chest beats, rips a slab from the ground and hoists it overhead (~1 s windup; a dashed trajectory arc to the target/aim point). Hurls a **projectile** (arcing, ~18 m/s): first fighter it touches takes ~200 + stagger 0.8 s, 60 splash in 2.5 m, breaks crates; dodgeable by moving/jumping/flying. | Crocodile | **Death Roll** (reworked) | lock, 7 m | Low burst lunge (path line); clamp; drag and 3 full death-roll revolutions thrashing the victim (grab, ~300 total over ~2.5 s, victim stunned, croc −50% damage); ends with a toss. Whiff = 1 s slide recovery. |

Ends behind the target, ~1 s recovery. Shadow marks show the blink positions. |

**AI:** bots must respect the new rules: `lock` ults are only cast with a valid target (use the same selection), L3/L4 read committed reticles/zones (`ultimateTarget`/telegraph data, reaction-delayed) and sidestep them; L1 casts eagerly but never dodges; Apex uses ults on staggered/guard-broken/isolated targets as before. Bots avoid standing in the mole vortex/hippo mud (soft) like traps.

## §5 First-person mode (WP-Q)
- **Toggle** with `V` in a match (also Settings → View: Third / First person; persisted `gk-settings.view`; FP FOV slider default 85°). Third-person stays default. Spectating stays third-person.
- **Camera:** anchored at each animal's eye point (per-animal profile `eye {forward, up, side}` in new files `src/render/animals/fp/<animal>.ts`, owned by WP-Q), driven by mouse yaw/pitch only — **never inherits body roll/pitch/spin** (rigs may flip/spin during ultimates; the FP camera must stay stable). Slight run bob/sway is fine, off during attacks. Near-plane small enough not to clip the own model.
- **Own rig in FP:** hide head/neck/mane/ears etc. per animal profile (`hide: string[]` of mesh/joint names or a render-layer approach); keep visible what a first-person animal sees: forelegs/paws/claws swinging in view, croc/hippo jaws at screen bottom/top, snout tip, eagle wings/talons, giraffe neck hidden but hooves kicking, python coils below, mole claws. Shadows still cast. Attack/block/special/glide/soar/hit-flinch/knockdown animations must read well from inside the head: exaggerate paw travel into view where needed (FP-only pose adjustments live in the FP profile, not in the shared third-person poses), keep the camera steady on hit reactions (use a small view kick instead of body spin), grabbed/knocked-down/dead states switch to a sensible view (e.g. look at the attacker, or fall back to third-person for the death cam).
- **Systems adapted:** lock-on works (yaw eases toward the target), attack range indicator / ult preview drawn from eye-height view (ground decals fine), nameplates/threat arrows/damage wedge unchanged, crosshair dot (settings toggle), pointer lock as before, `aimYaw` = camera yaw, eagle climbs with pitch-independent Space as today, aim assist unchanged. The player's own effects that would obscure the view (fire trap flames, big VFX at the feet) should be readable but not opaque; fade own-body-attached effects that intersect the camera.
- **Ultimate FP pass (later phase):** when the new ultimates exist, each needs a checked FP behaviour (see §6 Phase 3): e.g. lion leap keeps looking at the target; croc roll doesn't spin the camera; eagle dive looks down at the reticle; panther blinks snap smoothly; python tether visible from the eyes.
