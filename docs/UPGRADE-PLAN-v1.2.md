## §0 The request (verbatim intent)
1. Fix the eagle's glide animation — wings do not stretch out and it looks funny.
2. Let the eagle fly higher so it can dodge attacks. At max height it cannot attack, but it deals AoE damage when it lands (not too much — must not be OP).
3. Make the power-up icons (heal/speed/rage pickups) much more obviously readable as what they are.
4. Add random traps around the arena, scaled by the selected difficulty. Traps deal damage but must not hand anyone a big advantage. Examples: a fire pit that erupts when stepped on, or spikes. Each is active for **8 seconds** after being triggered and damages **everyone who is inside while it is active, including the fighter who triggered it**. **Blocking must not negate trap damage.**

## §1 Shared contract (ALREADY in `src/core/types.ts` — do not change without telling the architect)
- `TrapKind = 'fire' | 'spikes'`, `TrapPhase = 'armed' | 'active' | 'cooldown'`.
- `TrapState { id, kind, pos, radius, phase, timeLeft, triggeredBy }` and `WorldSnapshot.traps: TrapState[]` (currently stubbed as `[]` in `World.snapshot()` — WP-N implements it).
- New `GameEvent`s: `trapTriggered {trapId, kind, pos, fighterId}`, `trapDamage {trapId, kind, targetId, damage, pos}` (aggregated ~2×/s per victim, unblockable), `trapExpired {trapId, kind, pos}`, `landingImpact {fighterId, pos, radius, damage, height}` (eagle landing AoE, emitted once when the slam applies).
- `KillFeedEntry.cause?: 'trap'` in `src/ui/HUD.ts` (WP-O renders it; WP-P sets it when a death has `killerId === -1`).
- Death by a trap: `death` event with `killerId = -1` (environment). No kill credit to anyone.
- Difficulty (1–4) is already in `MatchConfig`; the sim now uses it to decide how many traps to place.

## §3 WP-N — sim rules

### 3.1 Eagle flight ("soar") — extends the existing glide (§7.8)
Today: airborne + hold Space → glide at fixed y≈1.6 for ≤2.5 s, ends when Space is released, 6 s cooldown. New behavior (same single input; `FighterIntent` unchanged):
- Hold Space in the air → glide as before **and keep holding → climb** smoothly toward `maxHeight` (~6.5 m, ≈4 m/s climb rate). Move at glide speed horizontally while climbing/hovering. Total flight time allowed goes up (~4 s) so the climb is worth it. Release Space (or run out of flight time) → controlled descent/fall to the ground.
- **Above `attackLockHeight` (≈3.2 m, i.e. clearly above glide altitude) the eagle cannot attack, use its special, or use its ultimate** (intents ignored; it can still move, and can still block? no — no block either while flying).
- **Dodging = vertical separation.** Melee arc tests already require |Δy| ≤ 2.2 m (3.2 giraffe) — keep that. Make every *ground* AoE / circle / cone in `abilities1.ts`, `abilities2.ts`, `CombatSystem` (Lion roar, Gorilla leap slam, Hippo chomp, Sinkhole, Coil Sweep, Giraffe spin, Rhino stampede, knockdown zones, pickups, traps…) ignore fighters whose altitude is above ~2.5 m (only fighters at ground level are hit). Eagle Death-From-Above (untargetable soar + dive) stays as is.
- **Landing slam.** When the eagle touches down after having been above `landMinHeight` (~3.0 m): AoE damage to grounded enemies within ~3.2 m: `base ≈ 30 + ≈4 per metre of height fallen`, capped ≈ 55; small knockback (~2 m); eagle suffers a short landing recovery (~0.4 s, cannot act) so it is a real trade-off; emit `landingImpact`. Must be clearly weaker than the eagle's Death From Above (240) and its Gale Burst (45) per use.
- Cooldown for the flight starts on landing (≈6–7 s). Keep `glideT`/`airborne`/`action:'glide'` semantics for the renderer (`state.pos.y` and `state.vel.y` describe altitude/climb; do NOT add required fields to `FighterState` — optional only).
- All numbers live in `src/config/animals.ts` (extend the eagle `glide` perk or add a `soar` block) and are documented in BLUEPRINT §7.8/§8 + the eagle's description strings/lore/ability text shown in the character select.

### 3.2 Traps
- **Config** `src/config/traps.ts` (all numbers here): `TRAP_COUNT_BY_DIFFICULTY` — start at `{1: 2, 2: 3, 3: 5, 4: 7}` (higher difficulty = more traps); `ACTIVE_SECONDS = 8` exactly; re-arm cooldown after expiry ≈ 20 s; per-kind radius (fire ≈ 2.0 m, spikes ≈ 1.8 m; trigger when a grounded fighter's centre is within the radius of an armed plate); damage per kind (start: fire ≈ 12/s as a burn, spikes ≈ 20 per hit with a per-victim re-hit interval ≈ 0.8 s). **Tune with data** (see acceptance).
- **Sim** `src/sim/TrapSystem.ts`, ticked by `World` (before/after status effects — your call, keep determinism). Placement is seeded (`mulberry32(seed ^ constant)`), decided at match start: radius 6–25 m from centre; clear of obstacles (pillars, fallen columns, crates, dais) by ≥ 1.5 m, pickup pads ≥ 2.5 m, spawn points ≥ 3.5 m, other traps ≥ 6 m; kind chosen randomly per trap (both kinds should appear); place fewer if it cannot satisfy constraints. Traps are visible to everyone (a pressure plate), so bots may use the snapshot.
- Trigger: armed + a **grounded**, alive, non-burrowed, non-untargetable fighter enters the radius (a fighter landing on it triggers it too; jumping over / gliding / eagle soaring does not). Then `phase='active'` for exactly 8 s: **every grounded fighter inside the radius takes damage, including the triggerer** (the triggerer must take damage the moment they trigger it, not be exempt). Airborne/soaring/burrowed fighters are unaffected.
- **Damage is unblockable and true**: ignores block, guard, armour buffs and never drains guard — like bleed. It does not trigger flinch/stagger, does not grant ult charge to anyone; the victim's "damage taken → ult charge" comeback lever should NOT apply (or applies at most in a documented, tiny way — prefer not). Emit `trapDamage` aggregated ≈ every 0.5 s per victim (not per tick). Bloodlust multiplier does not apply.
- Deaths from traps: `killerId = -1`, normal placement/`death` event, no kill credit.
- After 8 s: emit `trapExpired`, `phase='cooldown'`, re-arm after the cooldown (`armed` again).
- `snapshot().traps` must be an accurate deep copy each snapshot (double-buffer/alloc rules of the existing snapshot code apply).
- **Bots (`src/ai`)**: read `snapshot.traps` only. L1 Cub ignores traps; L2 avoids walking onto armed plates when convenient and out of active hazards; L3/L4 route around armed plates, never stand in active hazards, and leave one quickly if they end up in it; L4 may (optionally) deliberately fight near/lure into active traps but must never chase a plate-trigger suicidally. Eagle bots: use the soar defensively (Veteran/Apex when low HP or when an enemy telegraph is incoming, then land on enemies), and always release jump so they don't hover forever.
- Update the difficulty card copy in `src/config/botProfiles.ts` (each level's description/bullets) to mention the number of arena traps.

## §5b Hitbox fidelity — "what you see is what you hit" (added mid-flight; user report: Lion and Mole basic attacks don't damage inside the full visible hitbox / range feels unclear and inconsistent)
**Diagnosis (architect):** (1) `MatchController` draws every swing ribbon with a fixed `SWING_RANGE = 2.4`, `SWING_ARC_DEG = 110`, but the sim uses each animal's own `range`/`arcDeg` (lion 2.2/120°, mole 2.0/120°) — the mole's drawn reach is ~20% longer than its real reach; (2) `meleeArcHit` (src/sim/hitbox.ts) tests the **target's centre** against the range and arc with no allowance for its body, so real reach depends on the target's size (a hippo whose body is inside the drawn arc is missed if its centre is outside; a mole is much easier to hit) and the arc's angular edge cuts through bodies; (3) the ribbon spawns at swing **start** while damage is evaluated ~0.4 s later at the impact instant (55%) using the fighter's yaw *then*, so movement in between makes the arc lie.
**Spec:**
- **WP-N (sim):** make the basic melee test a *sector–circle overlap*: hit iff `dist − target.radius ≤ range` (bodies that overlap the attacker's centre also count) AND `|angle to target centre| ≤ arcDeg/2 + asin(min(1, target.radius / dist))`, plus the existing height tolerance and targetability. Give `coneHit` the same angular padding so ability cones agree. Because effective reach grows by the target's radius, **retune every animal's `range`** (start from `oldRange − ~0.6…0.75`; final values by sweep) so relative reaches (giraffe > python > hippo/rhino > … > mole) and win-rate bands from §3.3 hold; update `statPips`, lore/ability text, BLUEPRINT §7.3/§8, bot spacing/"in range" logic in `src/ai` (must now add the target's radius), and the crate check. Emit **`swingImpact`** (already in `core/types.ts`) at the impact tick of every basic swing with the exact `pos`, actual `yaw`, `range`, `arcDeg`, `step` used for the test (gorilla's finisher slam: `arcDeg: 360`, `range` = slam radius). Tests: body-overlap hits at edge/miss just outside for radii 0.5 (mole), 0.7 (lion), 1.2 (hippo); lion + mole regressions across several target sizes and angles; `swingImpact` payload equals the tested sector; sweep still in band with 0 timeouts.
- **WP-P (render/match):** stop drawing the fixed 2.4/110 ribbon. Draw the sector from `swingImpact` (exact `range`/`arcDeg`/`yaw`/`pos`, at the impact instant; friendly colour for the player). Keep the whoosh sound at swing start. Add a subtle **attack-range indicator** for the player's own fighter: a faint ground sector in front of the fighter using the animal's real `ANIMALS[animal].range`/`arcDeg` read from config at runtime (config values are being retuned), oriented by the fighter's interpolated yaw, shown only when an enemy is within ~1.6× range or during a swing, brightening at impact; Settings toggle "Attack range indicator" (On/Off; `gk-settings.rangeIndicator`, default on; minimal edits to `src/ui/SettingsPanel.ts` + `src/ui/storage.ts`, which are free). Verify visually that the drawn sector and actual hits agree for Lion and Mole.
